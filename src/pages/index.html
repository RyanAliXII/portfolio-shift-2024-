About Me

Hi! I'm Ryan Ali. My journey in tech began in 2017 when I pursued the ICT strand in Senior High School, focusing on IT in Mobile Application and Web Development. This stemmed from my curiosity about computer programming. During that time, I gained hands-on experience building Web, Desktop, and Mobile applications .

While working on my degree in Computer Science, I joined a company specializing in IoT (Internet of Things) technology and also took on freelance work. These opportunities helped me apply my knowledge to real-world projects and sharpen my skills.

After graduating, I began working at Cignal TV, the Philippines’ premier DTH satellite provider, providing premium TV content to households and establishments nationwide, as an Application Developer. I'm currently working with .NET technologies, focusing on migrating legacy systems from .NET Framework to .NET Core and modernizing existing codebases to improve maintainability and performance.

In my free time, I enjoy going to the gym and discovering new local places near me, and hopefully soon, I'll be able to travel to farther destinations as well.
